A Virtual Trucking Company (VTC) or Virtual Bus Company (VBC) is your organization's home on TrucklineMP. This guide walks through eligibility, the creation wizard, and what happens after your company goes live.

## Before you start

You need:

- A signed-in TrucklineMP account
- Permission to create a VTC/VBC (platform eligibility rules apply)
- No existing company of the same type where you are already the owner (one VTC and one VBC ownership per account)

Open the creation flow at [trucklinemp.com/vtc/create](https://trucklinemp.com/vtc/create) for VTCs, or [trucklinemp.com/vbc/create](https://trucklinemp.com/vbc/create) for VBCs.

---

## Eligibility check

When you open the create page, TrucklineMP runs an eligibility check automatically. If you are not eligible, the page explains why. Common reasons include:

- Already owning a company of the requested type (VTC or VBC)
- Platform standing or restrictions (note: there is no minimum account age requirement to create a company)
- Feature disabled by platform configuration

You cannot proceed until eligibility passes.

## Creation wizard

The wizard has four steps: **Identity**, **Story**, **Presence**, and **Review**.

### Step 1: Identity

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Company Name** | Display name shown across the platform. Required. |
| **Handle** | Unique URL slug: `trucklinemp.com/vtc/@yourhandle` (for VTCs) or `trucklinemp.com/vbc/@yourhandle` (for VBCs). 2-15 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only. Required. |
| **Slogan** | Short tagline (optional). |
| **Primary Language** | Default language for your company. |
| **Games** | Select the simulators your company plays in. At least one is required (ETS2 or ATS). |
| **In-Game Tag** | Short tag used in-game, for example `TLP` (optional). |
| **Headquarters City** | Optional city where your company is based. |
| **Logo** | Square profile image. Paste an HTTPS image URL or upload a file (JPEG, PNG, or WebP). |
| **Banner** | Wide cover image. Paste an HTTPS image URL or upload a file. |

### Step 2: Story

Write your company story in Markdown. Required, up to 5,000 characters. This appears on your public profile.

### Step 3: Presence

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Visibility** | Public, unlisted, or private. See [Visibility](/web-docs/vtc/visibility/). |
| **Minimum Account Age** | Days a TrucklineMP account must exist before applying. Set `0` for no limit. |
| **Minimum Playtime** | Hours of playtime required to apply. Set `0` for no limit. |
| **Recruitment Open** | Whether applicants can apply immediately after creation. |
| **Contact Email** | Optional public contact address. |
| **Social Links** | Website, Discord invite, Instagram, Facebook, and X/Twitter. Must be full `https://` URLs if provided. |

### Step 4: Review

Review a live preview of your company profile, then submit. On success you are redirected to your new company profile.

## Default setup

When your company is created, TrucklineMP seeds default roles:

- **Management**
- **Staff**
- **Driver** (or **Driver/Coach** depending on VTC/VBC type)
- **Recruit**

You become the owner with full permissions. Open **Manage** from your company profile to configure recruitment, members, and Discord linking.

## Troubleshooting

### "Not eligible to create a company"

Read the reasons shown on the create page. You may already own a company of that type (VTC/VBC) or need to resolve a standing issue.

### Handle will not save

Handles must be 2-15 characters, lowercase, and use only letters, numbers, and underscores. The handle must be unique.

### Creation fails on social URLs

Every optional URL must start with `https://`. Discord invite links must be full URLs, not bare codes.
